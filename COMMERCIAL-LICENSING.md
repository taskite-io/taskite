# Taskite Commercial Licensing

The Taskite Community License permits non-commercial use without a license
fee. It does not permit commercial use.

A separate written commercial license is required when Taskite is used:

- in a product, paid service, hosted service, or managed service;
- for the internal production operations of a for-profit business;
- by a contractor or consultant for a client's commercial activity.

Commercial organizations may perform internal, non-production evaluation
solely to decide whether to obtain a commercial license. Evaluation must not
serve production workloads, customers, or revenue-generating activity. A
commercial license is required before moving beyond those limits.
