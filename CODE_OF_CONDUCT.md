# Taskite Community Code of Conduct

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Taskite repositories, issue trackers, review
systems, discussions, events, and private communications made in the course of
participating in the project. It applies to contributors, maintainers, users,
vendors, and other participants.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and work;
- welcome people regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability;
- give and receive constructive feedback;
- respect privacy, consent, and personal boundaries;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest; and
- accept reasonable moderation decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following conduct is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or stalking;
- sexualized language, imagery, or attention;
- insults, personal attacks, sustained disruption, trolling, or spamming;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- retaliation against a person who reports a concern; or
- conduct that would reasonably make another participant feel unsafe or
  unwelcome.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `lz19960321lz@gmail.com`. Include the
relevant links, dates, context, and any supporting material. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. A person named in a report
will not participate in deciding its outcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or
temporarily or permanently ban a participant. The response will reflect the
severity, frequency, and context of the conduct. Knowingly false reports or
retaliation may also result in enforcement.

This Code of Conduct governs community participation only. It does not change
the software license or grant rights to use Taskite.
